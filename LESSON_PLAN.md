# 45-Minute Lesson Protocol: TikTok Fame & Exotic Pets

* **Target Audience:** 4th Grade English Learners (CEFR A1+)
* **Main Concept:** Media Literacy & Wildlife Needs (Screen vs. Reality)
* **Key Vocabulary:** *Wetland, herd, chew, teeth, webbed feet, snorkel face, nightlight, wild, pet*
* **Core Structures:**
  * `An axolotl needs [cold water / clean tanks].`
  * `A capybara cannot live in a [bedroom].`
  * `Keep it wild! / Buy the toy!`

---

## 45-Minute Step-by-Step Delivery

### 1. Warm-Up & Hook (00:00 – 00:07)
* Open `slides.html` on Slide 1.
* Read the title: *"TikTok Fame & Exotic Pets: Real or Fake?"*
* **Teacher Prompt:** *"Who uses TikTok? Have you seen cute capybaras or smiling axolotls?"*
* **Student Output:** Hands up, short responses (*"Yes, very cute!"*).
* Jump to Slide 2 to present the 5 viral animals. Practice choral pronunciation of:
  * *Axolotl* (/ˈæk.sə.lɒt.əl/)
  * *Capybara* (/ˌkæp.ɪˈbɑː.rə/)

### 2. Physical TPR: The 3 Wild Superpowers (00:07 – 00:15)
Teach the three biological adaptations using physical actions:
1. **Snorkel Face:** Students crouch under desks and lift only their eyes and noses above desk level.  
   *Teacher:* *"Can you breathe? Can you see?"* ➔ *Students:* *"Yes, snorkel face!"*
2. **Webbed Feet:** Students spread their fingers wide and paddle the air.  
   *Teacher:* *"Paddle like flippers to escape jaguars!"*
3. **Herd Power:** Have students link arms with their desk neighbor.  
   *Teacher:* *"Capybaras stay together in herds of 20!"*

### 3. Video Evidence & Contrast (00:15 – 00:25)
* Switch from `slides.html` to `index.html` (Mission 1: Video Evidence Lounge).
* Play 20–30 seconds of each video:
  * YouTube clip 1: Point to the capybara gnawing wooden furniture. Ask: *"Why is it chewing the chair?"* Elicit: *"Front teeth never stop growing!"*
  * YouTube clip 2: Point to the capybaras diving into the river. Ask: *"Where is this?"* Elicit: *"Wetland / Brazil!"*
* Complete Mission 1 checks as a class.

### 4. Interactive Missions & Living Room Escape (00:25 – 00:38)
* Progress through Missions 2, 3, and 4 in `index.html`:
  * **Mission 2:** Call a student to the smartboard to match the Axolotl to cold water.
  * **Mission 3:** Have students vote with fingers: *"Option 2: Buy the nightlight!"*
  * **Mission 4:** As the whole class performs each TPR action (Snorkel Face, Webbed Feet, Herd), tap the corresponding adaptation on screen to watch the apartment transform into the green Pantanal wetland!

### 5. Speaking Drill & Exit Ticket (00:38 – 00:45)
* Switch to Slide 5 in `slides.html` for the Call-and-Response chant:
  * **Teacher:** *"Do you want an Axolotl in your bed?"*
  * **Class:** *"No! Buy a nightlight instead!"*
  * **Teacher:** *"Can a Capybara live in a flat?"*
  * **Class:** *"No! It chews the couch and needs a river habitat!"*
* **Exit Ticket:** To leave the classroom, each student raises their hand and completes one sentence:
  * *"A capybara needs [a river / a herd / grass]."*
