The 60-kg Hamster Dilemma

Why Capybaras cannot live in your house

❌ In an Apartment:

• Front teeth never stop growing (chews couches and tables!)
• Bathtub is way too small (needs 6 hrs in water)
• Smelly scent fluid on furniture
• Gets sad and sick alone

✔️ In South American Wetlands:

• Chews wild grasses and river stems
• Swims in deep rivers with webbed feet
• Snorkel face (eyes and nose stay above water)
• Herd of 20 friends protects baby pups
